# Initial Beneficiary Closure establishes beneficiary knowledge from complete evidence

Rules truth and live knowledge remain separate under ADR-0010. A Faction Agent Group Observation proves who participated in a collective Faction operation, but it neither identifies exact Roles nor by itself proves who benefits from that Faction. Immediately treating every observed Werewolf Agent as a Werewolf Beneficiary would be false for Agent-only exceptions such as White Werewolf or a temporary Werewolf Agent, while exactly identifying every ordinary Werewolf or asking for a separate Beneficiary roster would add information and Moderator work that the game does not require.

Initial Beneficiary Closure is a one-time automatic Night 1 rule boundary. It becomes eligible only after the complete initial Werewolf Faction Agent group and every committed fact needed to determine each initial Beneficiary exception reachable from the admitted Role Composition are available, and before any rule consumes the resulting Beneficiary facts. An incomplete prerequisite set makes the readiness check an atomic no-op; independent canonical interactions may continue, but reaching a Beneficiary-dependent consumer while a required fact remains unresolved is an invariant failure.

Core evaluates the committed facts at their effective chronological boundaries without publishing intermediate Known facts. Core first places every committed explicit Beneficiary fact, Agent-only exception, and transition at its effective historical boundary. For each Player still unresolved at the initial collective Werewolf boundary, membership in the complete observed initial Agent group supplies a Werewolf Beneficiary candidate, while absence supplies a Villager Beneficiary candidate. Deferred rules such as initial Lovers classification evaluate those candidates at the rule's original historical boundary. Choices and transitions committed later in Night 1 do not retroactively alter an earlier classification. Closure atomically appends one batch containing only newly entailed residual and deferred facts, each retaining its effective historical boundary. Previously committed explicit facts are neither rewritten nor re-appended. Projecting all committed facts in effective-boundary order yields the final Known Faction State.

Candidate values are never Known Faction facts. Closure identifies no exact Role, uses no remaining-card or simulator deduction, commits at most once, and is never reopened. Infection, Wild Child's Role-preserving Faction transition, Permanent Role Swap, and later effects are chronological transitions, not corrections. A Role Composition from which a new non-Villager Beneficiary or Agent-with-different-Beneficiary exception is reachable is not App-Supported until that Role's establishment path is available to closure. Angel is explicitly outside that category: its holder uses ordinary Villager Beneficiary mechanics throughout, and its physical-card-based victory eligibility and post-expiry Simple Villager mechanics neither add a closure prerequisite nor identify its holder.

Closure creates no Moderator Instruction, Moderator Response, or independent recovery checkpoint. The response or automatic transition that supplies the final prerequisite commits that prerequisite, one closure batch containing only newly entailed residual and deferred facts at their effective historical boundaries, and the next Pending Instruction at one ordinary stable boundary. Rehydration can observe the pre-closure state and deterministically run the readiness check again, or the complete post-closure state, but never a partially closed or provisionally corrected state.

## Amendment: validate complete initial Agent-group cardinality

At the initial all-alive Werewolf collective boundary, after earlier same-night effects have committed, Core derives the exact number of living Werewolf Faction Agents from living Players already committed as Agents plus active agency-capable cards not already accounted to a committed Agent. If Core cannot prove how a committed Agent relates to the active agency-capable cards, it fails closed rather than guessing whether that Agent consumes a card. That cardinality constrains the private Faction Agent Group Observation to exactly that many Players. The Moderator's observation, not the derivation, establishes every Player-specific Agent or non-Agent fact; the constraint establishes no exact Role, identity, Beneficiary, public knowledge, or durable count or provenance fact.

This derivation is exact only at that all-alive boundary. If a dead or otherwise unresolved seat makes the calculation merely an upper bound, Core neither offers nor accepts this exhaustive observation and commits nothing; supporting that state requires a separate recovery design. At the valid boundary, a wrong-sized response is rejected before mutation and leaves the same instruction pending. A physical wake-group discrepancy fails closed without a Moderator override or automatic correction of the Role Composition or committed facts. The exact count is shown only on the Moderator-private instruction; neither the count nor its provenance is exposed publicly.

## Considered options

- **Assign every observed Werewolf Agent a provisional Werewolf Beneficiary and correct exceptions later**: rejected because accepted facts are immutable, recovery would expose a false intermediate Known state, and dependent rules could consume it.
- **Exactly identify every Werewolf Role**: rejected because ordinary Werewolf identity is unnecessary for Beneficiary resolution and the extra observation would disclose and record more than the physical flow requires.
- **Treat Angel as an Initial Beneficiary Closure exception**: rejected because the Angel holder uses ordinary Villager Beneficiary mechanics; Angel's win eligibility and expiry are physical-role-card lifecycle rules, not Faction knowledge.
- **Ask the Moderator for a complete Beneficiary roster**: rejected because Beneficiary resolution is a Core-owned consequence of already committed table facts, not another physical choice or observation.
- **Leave residual Beneficiaries Unknown after all exceptions are resolved**: rejected because it would block Lovers classification and Victory Check Windows despite the rules and committed evidence already entailing the answer.
- **Accept a partial nonempty Agent group**: rejected because this observation commits an exhaustive living partition and Initial Beneficiary Closure gives absence meaning. An under-count would therefore create durable false Agent and Beneficiary knowledge rather than preserve uncertainty.
- **Treat the roster-derived capacity as universally exact**: rejected because a dead unresolved seat can make an active agency-capable card an upper bound rather than a living-Agent count.
- **Let the Moderator override a cardinality discrepancy**: rejected for this boundary because the current model has no coherent way to repair the locked Role Composition and already committed consequences. A recoverable override requires a separate decision and state transition.
