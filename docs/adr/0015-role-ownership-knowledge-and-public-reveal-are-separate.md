# Role ownership, Moderator knowledge, and public reveal are separate

A Player's Physical Character Card Ownership, current Role, whether the Moderator/app knows that Role, and whether Players have publicly seen it are separate facts. The Physical Deal initially establishes card ownership and the printed Role supplies the initial current Role. A Permanent Role Swap changes the current Role and must separately define physical-card handling and visibility. Role Identification records a private exact-Role observation; it does not deal or assign a card. Faction Agent Group Observation records an observed Faction group without identifying exact Roles. Role Reveal records a public physical event and does not change the current Role.

An unidentified Role is not implicitly Simple Villager and supplies no guessed Faction, trigger, or outcome fact. Moderator-only state may durably retain exact private Roles, choices, Faction state, and Status Effects once legitimately observed or explicitly committed by a rules transition. It never deduces an unknown Role or Faction state merely from remaining inventory. Public announcements and public-history projections use only publicly revealed facts and preserve earlier public history. [ADR-0010](./0010-faction-model-separates-beneficiaries-from-agents.md) defines Faction-fact readiness.

Deterministic entailment from the complete committed fact set plus the locked Role Composition is permitted. As in [ADR-0019](./0019-initial-beneficiary-closure-establishes-beneficiary-knowledge.md), it requires complete committed prerequisites and an entailed result; provisional conclusions and later corrections are forbidden. This exception does not permit gap-filling, guessing, or probabilistic inference from remaining inventory.

A public Role Reveal always commits a public-knowledge event, even if the current Role was already Moderator-known. When the Role is already known, the Moderator acknowledges that the physical reveal occurred; when it is unknown, the Moderator supplies a complete valid mapping for every requested Player. The response is validated against Role Composition multiplicity, known ownership, public history, and card zones. When multiple unused cards print the mapped Role, they are interchangeable for Player ownership: Core may deterministically bind any unused matching stable card ID. That ID is an accounting handle, not a claim that the Moderator distinguished an identical physical copy; configured Offer 1 and Offer 2 slots remain distinct.

The [Moderator submission and commitment flow](../contracts/moderator-interaction.md#submission-and-commitment) defines acceptance, correction, and recovery for these responses.

For a pending Dawn or Vote victim, an explicit pre-reveal interception runs first, generic Role Reveal commits next, and the actual Elimination or rule-defined replacement commits only after reveal. Every resulting reaction drains in the same Elimination Cascade before navigation.

Simulation Start State is omniscient run-level truth. The headless driver answers identification and reveal instructions from that seeded truth and later swaps, rather than randomly assigning a remaining Role when asked. Ordinary simulated decisions must not use hidden assignments as strategy information.
